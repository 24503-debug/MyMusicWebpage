Hip hop

The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill (1998) is a groundbreaking album that flawlessly blends hip-hop, R&B, neo-soul, and reggae. Lauryn Hill's intricate rapping and soulful vocals explore personal heartbreak, motherhood, spirituality, and self-empowerment, making it one of the most celebrated projects in music history.

Nas's 1994 debut album, Illmatic, is widely considered the pinnacle of East Coast hip-hop. With vivid lyricism, cinematic storytelling, and legendary production from DJ Premier, Large Professor, and Pete Rock, Nas paints an unforgettable portrait of youth and survival in Queensbridge, New York.
